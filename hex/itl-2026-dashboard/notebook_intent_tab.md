# Intent

Website demand around the conference — not Fliplet visits, not app.n8n.cloud.

## What these numbers mean

- **User** = a distinct browser that loaded a page. **Event** = one tracked hit (page view, click). These people were on the website, not necessarily in the room.
- **Two clocks, two site sets.** The week comparison is **n8n.io only**, Monday–Sunday. The Jun–Nov tiles are public n8n sites together: n8n.io, docs, community, blog, learn, plans.
- Berlin events use `geo_city = Berlin`. Germany / Europe on the long view use country and continent. None of this is door scans.

## Did the conference week spike?

Compare **12–18 Oct** with the week before, **5–11 Oct**, on n8n.io.

| | Change | 5–11 Oct | 12–18 Oct conference | 19–25 Oct after | Read as |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Berlin events | **+33%** | 8,601 | 11,428 | 6,995 | This is the spike. Then it fell back. |
| n8n.io users worldwide | +1% | 173,571 | 174,802 | 186,284 | No global spike in the week |
| CTA clicks | −3% | 80,954 | 78,744 | 68,808 | No conference lift on Get started |
| Trial starts | −1% | 417 | 414 | 286 | No conference lift on trials |
| Sales forms | ~flat | 175 | 180 | 132 | Forms stayed ~flat in the week |

The story: Berlin spiked. The rest of n8n.io did not. After the event, Berlin dropped and worldwide users rose slightly (186,284).
